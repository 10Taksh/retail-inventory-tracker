  1. A PDF lands in the invoices folder or is uploaded.
  2. The text is read and Gemini returns each product with its expiry date as JSON.
  3. Products are saved to SQLite; a new delivery replaces the old expiry date.
  4. The dashboard flags expired and soon-to-expire stock.
